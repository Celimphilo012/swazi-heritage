01

Platform Overview & Roles

The Swazi Cultural Heritage Platform is a digital preservation system for the Kingdom of Eswatini's ceremonies, royal lineages, music, and cultural traditions. It serves four distinct user types, each with their own workspace and capabilities.

Role Who They Are What They Can Do Home URL
Public Unauthenticated visitors Browse ceremonies, lineage records, marketplace; view the homepage /
User Registered general public Everything public + AI chat, cinema, bookings, ratings, personalised recommendations /
Ceremony Keeper Registered cultural practitioner Create & manage ceremonies, songs, walkthrough steps, offer services /practitioner
History Keeper Registered cultural practitioner Create & manage lineage records, clan trees, offer services /practitioner
Admin Platform administrator Review & approve content, manage users, cinema, analytics, system config /admin
ℹ️
How roles are assigned Regular self-registration always creates a User account. Practitioner and Admin roles are assigned by an existing administrator from the User Management panel.
02

Getting Started

Creating an Account

1
Navigate to the registration page

Open the platform in your browser and click Sign In in the top-right navigation bar, then click Create account below the login form. Alternatively visit /register directly.

2
Fill in your details

Enter your full name, a valid email address, and a password. Passwords must be at least 8 characters. All three fields are required.

3
Submit and start exploring

Click Register. You are automatically logged in and redirected to the home page as a User.

Logging In

1
Go to the login page

Click Sign In in the navigation bar or visit /login.

2
Enter your credentials

Type your registered email and password, then click Sign in.

3
You are redirected to your role's home

Users land on the main homepage, practitioners land on /practitioner, and admins land on /admin.

⚠️
Forgot your password? Contact the platform administrator to have your account reset. Self-service password reset is handled through the admin's User Management panel.

Signing Out

Click the Sign out button found in the top-right navigation bar (desktop) or in the mobile drawer menu. Practitioner and Admin users click Sign out at the bottom of their sidebar.

03

Public Visitor — Browsing Without an Account

No account is required to read published cultural content. The following features are available to anyone who opens the platform.

🏛️
Homepage

View platform statistics (total ceremonies, lineage records, founding year), featured ceremonies, royal lineage highlights, and the Discover More section.

🔍
Explore Culture

Browse all published ceremonies with search and category filters. Click any ceremony card to open its full detail page.

📜
Lineage Records

Read published royal lineage and clan history records. Navigate from the Explore page's Lineage tab.

🛒
Marketplace

Browse services offered by cultural practitioners — performances, crafts, teaching, and more. Filter by category.

🎬
Cultural Cinema

See the schedule of upcoming live cultural streams and browse available recorded sessions.

⭐
Ratings Summary

View community star ratings and reviews for any ceremony or lineage record without being logged in.

🎓
Seminars & Workshops

Browse online and in-person cultural workshops hosted by practitioners, with a live map of physical venues.

👘
Imvunulo Catalogue

Browse traditional attire listed for rental or sale, with a map showing where sellers are located.

🗺️
Tourism

Discover heritage sites, lodges, cultural villages, and nature reserves across Eswatini on an interactive map.

📚
Library & Publications

Search articles, books, papers, and theses on Swazi history and culture, with relevance-ranked full-text search.

🔒
Gated features AI Cultural Chat, cinema bookings, seminar bookings, submitting ratings, sending marketplace or imvunulo enquiries, and setting personal preferences all require a registered account. A Sign In prompt appears when you try to access these features. Tourism and the Library are browsable by everyone, no account required.

Reading a Ceremony Detail Page

Click any ceremony card on Explore or the homepage to open its detail page. The page is organised into tabs:

TabWhat You See
OverviewFull description, siSwati translation, month celebrated, health/immunology notes, and GPS location if available.
SongsTraditional songs associated with this ceremony — title, artist, lyrics, and audio player if a recording is attached.
Attire (Imvunulo)Traditional dress items worn during the ceremony with images and descriptions.
WalkthroughNumbered step-by-step guide through the ceremony sequence (shown only if steps have been added by the practitioner).

Below the tabs you will find the Community Ratings section showing the average star rating and individual reviews.

04

Regular User — Full Feature Access

A User account unlocks every feature available to public visitors plus personalisation, community participation, and media features.

Home Page & Personalised Recommendations

After logging in, your homepage shows a Recommended for You section above the Featured Ceremonies list. Recommendations combine three signals: ceremonies and lineage records matched to your cultural interests, imvunulo listings matched to your budget, and tourist sites matched to your interests (shown on the Tourism page). If you have not set interests or a budget yet, a prompt guides you to the preferences section.

✅
Tip: Set your interests for better recommendations Go to Profile → Cultural Interests & Preferences and select the categories that interest you. Recommendations update immediately.

Setting Cultural Preferences

Access your profile by clicking your name/avatar in the top-right navigation and selecting the dashboard link, then navigating to Profile Settings. Or visit /profile directly.

1
Scroll to "Cultural Interests & Preferences"

This section appears below the account settings on the profile page.

2
Toggle your interest categories

Click any of the six category pills to select or deselect it: Ceremonies, Lineage, Music, Attire, Royal History, Spiritual. Selected categories turn gold.

3
Set an imvunulo budget (optional)

Enter a maximum amount in SZL in the Imvunulo budget field. Imvunulo listings priced at or below this amount will appear in your homepage recommendations. Leave it blank to skip budget-based recommendations.

4
Set your visitor type and preferred language

Choose Local, Tourist, or Researcher from the Visitor Type dropdown, and English or SiSwati from the Preferred Language dropdown.

5
Click "Save Preferences"

A confirmation message appears and your homepage recommendations refresh immediately.

Rating a Ceremony or Lineage Record

1
Open any ceremony or lineage record detail page

Navigate to Explore and click on any published entry.

2
Scroll to the Community Ratings section

Below the content tabs you will see the average star rating, a rating distribution bar chart, and existing reviews.

3
Click your star rating (1–5)

The interactive star row is shown for logged-in users. Click the star that matches your rating. Optionally type a comment in the text box.

4
Click "Submit Rating"

Your rating is saved. You can update it later — submitting again updates your existing rating rather than adding a duplicate.

AI Cultural Chat

Navigate to AI Chat in the top navigation (visible only when logged in). Type any question about Swazi culture, ceremonies, traditions, royal history, or the siSwati language. The AI assistant responds using the platform's cultural knowledge base powered by Google Gemini.

⚠️
Context-aware responses The chat assistant is configured for Swazi cultural topics. Off-topic questions may receive limited responses.

Cultural Cinema

Navigate to Cinema in the top navigation to browse available live streams and recorded cultural events.

TypeHow to Access
Live Sessions Sessions show as cards with a scheduled date/time. Click Book a Seat to reserve your place. You receive confirmation on screen. Booked sessions appear under My Bookings.
Recorded Videos Past sessions with an available video are marked as Recorded. Click the card to open the cinema room and watch the embedded video.

My Bookings

Click your avatar → dashboard, then navigate to My Bookings (or visit /my-bookings). This page lists all your current and past cinema seat bookings. You can cancel upcoming bookings from here.

Marketplace — Sending a Service Enquiry

Navigate to Marketplace in the top navigation to browse services offered by practitioners.

1
Browse and filter

Use the category pill filters at the top (All, Performance, Crafts, Teaching, Ceremony, Attire, Music, Other) to narrow the listing.

2
Click "Enquire" on a service card

An enquiry modal opens showing the service name, practitioner name, and price details.

3
Type your message and click "Send Enquiry"

The practitioner is emailed your message immediately, and a conversation thread is created. You will see a success confirmation in the modal with a link to My Enquiries.

4
Continue the conversation in "My Enquiries"

Visit /my-enquiries (or click your avatar → My Enquiries) to see every conversation you've started, split into a Marketplace tab and an Imvunulo tab. Select a conversation on the left to read replies and send follow-up messages in the chat panel on the right — no need to use email.

ℹ️
Account required for enquiries You must be logged in to send a marketplace or imvunulo enquiry, and you cannot enquire about your own listing. Public visitors can browse but cannot contact sellers.
05

Seminars, Imvunulo, Tourism & Library

Four platform-wide features available to any logged-in User (browsing is open to everyone; booking, enquiring, and submitting content requires an account).

Booking a Seminar or Workshop

Navigate to Seminars in the top navigation (/seminars) to browse workshops scheduled by practitioners.

1
Filter by format

Use the All / Online / In person pills to narrow the list. Seminars with a physical location appear as pins on the map above the list.

2
Click "Book a spot"

If the seminar has limited capacity and is full, the button is replaced with Fully booked. Otherwise your seat is confirmed immediately.

3
Find your booking under "My Bookings"

Visit /my-bookings and switch to the Seminars tab. Online seminars show a Join link button once the session starts; you can cancel an upcoming booking from here.

🔔
You'll be notified of changes If a practitioner reschedules or cancels a seminar you've booked, you receive both an in-app notification (bell icon, top of the page) and an email.

Renting or Buying Imvunulo

Navigate to Imvunulo in the top navigation (/imvunulo) to browse traditional attire listed for rental or sale.

1
Check the map for sellers near you

Listings with a pickup location on file appear as pins. A counter above the map shows how many of the current listings have a location set.

2
Filter by type and gender

Use the For sale / For rental and gender pills to narrow results.

3
Click "Send Enquiry"

Message the seller directly about sizing, availability, or pickup. Track the conversation under My Enquiries → Imvunulo.

Discovering Tourist Sites

Navigate to Tourism in the top navigation (/tourism) to discover heritage sites, lodges, cultural villages, nature reserves, and restaurants across Eswatini.

  • The map is the primary way to browse — every site with a location appears as a pin you can click for details.
  • If you're logged in and have set cultural interests in your profile, a Recommended for you row appears above the map, matched to those interests.
  • Filter the list below the map by category, and use the price range and contact details shown on each card to plan a visit.

Searching the Library

Navigate to Library in the top navigation (/library) — no account required. This works like a small-scale Google Scholar for Swazi history and culture.

1
Type a search term

The search bar searches titles, authors, abstracts, and keywords together and ranks results by relevance, not just keyword matches.

2
Filter by type

Narrow results to Articles, Books, Papers, or Theses using the filter pills.

3
Click a result to expand it

Read the full abstract and keywords. Click Cite to copy a ready-to-paste citation to your clipboard, or Read full text if the author attached a PDF or external link.

06

Ceremony Keeper — Managing Ceremonies

A Ceremony Keeper has access to a dedicated practitioner workspace at /practitioner. This role is assigned by an administrator. The left sidebar provides navigation to all tools.

Practitioner Dashboard Overview

The Overview page (/practitioner) shows:

  • A count of your published, pending, and rejected ceremonies
  • Shortcuts to create new content
  • A notification badge in the sidebar showing any content that has been reviewed

Creating a Ceremony

1
Click "Ceremonies" in the sidebar, then "New Ceremony"

This opens the ceremony creation form at /practitioner/ceremonies/new.

2
Fill in the ceremony details

Required fields: Name, Description, Month Celebrated. Optional fields: siSwati name and description, health/immunology notes, GPS location, and Category (used for recommendations — choose one of: Ceremonies, Lineage, Music, Attire, Royal, Spiritual).

3
Click "Submit for Review"

The ceremony is saved with pending_review status and sent to admins for approval. You cannot publish directly — all content requires admin approval.

⚠️
Editing published or rejected content resets status If you edit a ceremony that has already been published or rejected, its status automatically reverts to pending_review so the admin can re-evaluate the changes.

Managing Existing Ceremonies

The Ceremonies list page shows all your submissions with their current status badge (Pending, Published, Rejected). From each row you can:

  • Edit — open the ceremony form with pre-filled values
  • Manage Walkthrough — add/edit step-by-step walkthrough content (see below)
  • Delete — permanently remove the ceremony (confirmation required)

Walkthrough Manager

Navigate to any ceremony in the list and click Manage Walkthrough, or visit /practitioner/ceremonies/:id/walkthrough. This tool lets you build a numbered, step-by-step guide that users can follow on the ceremony's detail page.

1
Click "Add Step"

A form appears where you enter a title, description, and an optional image URL for that step.

2
Steps are automatically numbered

Each new step gets the next available number. Steps are displayed in order on the public ceremony detail page under the Walkthrough tab.

3
Edit or delete existing steps

Click the pencil icon on any step card to edit it inline. Click the trash icon to remove it.

Songs Library

Navigate to Songs Library in the sidebar. This page manages traditional songs associated with ceremonies. You can:

  • Add a new song with title, artist, language, lyrics, and an optional audio file URL
  • Link songs to specific ceremonies
  • Edit or delete existing songs

Notifications

The Notifications page (sidebar badge shows count) lists all ceremonies that have received a status change from the admin — published approvals and rejections with the admin's feedback note. Click any notification to open the related ceremony for editing.

07

History Keeper — Managing Lineage Records

A History Keeper shares the same practitioner workspace as Ceremony Keepers but manages lineage and clan content instead of ceremonies. The sidebar adapts to show history-specific tools.

Creating a Lineage Record

1
Click "Lineage Records" in the sidebar, then "New Record"

Opens the lineage creation form at /practitioner/lineage/new.

2
Fill in the record details

Fields include: Title, Content (rich text history), Clan/Kingdom, Time Period, Category (for recommendations), and optional siSwati translation fields.

3
Click "Submit for Review"

The record enters the admin review queue. The same approval workflow as ceremonies applies.

Clans Manager

Navigate to Clans in the sidebar (/practitioner/clans). This tool manages the Swazi clan structure — inkhundla groupings, totem animals, and interrelationships between clans. You can add, edit, and organise clan entries which appear in the public Lineage Explorer.

ℹ️
Notifications work identically for History Keepers The Notifications page shows admin decisions on submitted lineage records, with the rejection reason visible when applicable.
08

Practitioner Shared Features

Both Ceremony Keepers and History Keepers have access to these shared tools in the practitioner sidebar.

My Services — Listing Cultural Services

Navigate to My Services in the sidebar (/practitioner/services). Practitioners can advertise cultural services that users can discover in the public Marketplace.

Creating a Service

1
Click "Add New Service"

A service creation form slides open.

2
Fill in service details

Required: Title, Description, Category (Performance, Crafts, Teaching, Ceremony, Attire, Music, Other), Price / Rate. Optional: an image URL for the service card.

3
Click "Create Service"

The service is immediately live in the public Marketplace. No admin approval is required for services.

Editing or Deleting a Service

Your services list shows all active listings. Click the Edit (pencil) icon to update details. Click the Delete (trash) icon to remove a listing — a confirmation prompt appears before deletion.

Receiving Enquiries

When a user sends an enquiry on one of your services, you're emailed at your registered address and the enquiry appears under Reviews & Enquiries in your sidebar. Click Reply on the enquiry card to open an inline chat thread and respond without leaving the platform — no need to reply by email.

Seminars & Workshops

Navigate to Seminars in the sidebar (/practitioner/seminars) to schedule online or in-person workshops that users can book.

1
Click "New seminar"

Fill in a title, description, date & time, duration, and an optional capacity (leave blank for unlimited spots).

2
Choose a format

For Online, provide a meeting link. For In person, type a place name into the location field — suggestions appear as you type — and its coordinates are filled in automatically, or use the Use my current location button.

3
Manage bookings

Click Attendees on any seminar to see who has booked. Editing the date, time, or format after people have booked notifies every attendee automatically (bell + email). Cancel does the same.

Imvunulo Listings

Navigate to Imvunulo Listings in the sidebar (/practitioner/imvunulo) to list traditional attire for rental or sale.

1
Click "New listing"

Fill in a title, description, listing type (sale, rental, or both), gender, and a price with an optional unit (e.g. "per day").

2
Add a pickup location (recommended)

Listings without a location don't appear on the buyer-facing map, so type a place name to get suggestions, or use your current location.

3
Respond to enquiries

Buyer messages appear under Reviews & Enquiries and trigger both a bell notification and an email. Reply inline the same way as service enquiries.

Publications

Navigate to Publications in the sidebar (/practitioner/library) to contribute to the Library. Both Ceremony Keepers and History Keepers can submit.

1
Click "Submit publication"

Provide a title, authors, abstract, type (article, book, paper, or thesis), year, and keywords — keywords improve how well your work is found in Library search.

2
Attach the full text (optional)

Paste a link or upload a PDF directly.

3
Submit for review

Like ceremonies and lineage records, publications go to the admin review queue before appearing in the public Library. Editing a published or rejected publication resubmits it for review.

Profile Settings

Navigate to Profile in the sidebar or click your name at the bottom of the sidebar (/practitioner/profile). Here you can:

  • Update your display name and email
  • Change your password (enter current password + new password)
  • Set your cultural interests, imvunulo budget, and preferences (same interface as regular users)

Administrator — Platform Management

Administrators access a dedicated dashboard at /admin with a dark sidebar organising all management tools into two groups: Management and System.

Default Admin Email
admin@swazi.heritage
Default Password
Admin@1234
Change on first login

Overview Dashboard

The Admin Overview (/admin) shows platform-wide statistics: total users, ceremonies, lineage records, and activity over time. A badge on the Content Review nav item shows how many submissions are awaiting approval.

User Management

Navigate to Users (/admin/users). This page lists all registered accounts with search and filter options.

ActionHow
Change RoleClick the role dropdown on any user row. Select the new role (user, history_keeper, ceremony_keeper, admin). Changes take effect immediately.
Deactivate AccountToggle the active/inactive switch. Deactivated users cannot log in.
Reset PasswordClick the reset icon to generate a temporary password for the user.
Delete UserClick the delete icon. A confirmation prompt appears. Deletion is permanent.

Content Review

Navigate to Content Review (/admin/review). This is the primary moderation queue, split into three tabs — Ceremonies, Lineage Records, and Publications — each showing practitioner submissions with pending_review status by default (use the status dropdown to see published or rejected items instead).

1
Select a submission to review

The queue lists submissions ordered by date. Click any row to expand the full content, including siSwati translations and notes.

2
Approve or Reject

Click Approve to publish the content immediately (status → published). Click Reject to return it to the practitioner — you must enter a rejection reason that the practitioner will see in their Notifications.

3
Practitioner is notified

The practitioner sees the decision in their Notifications page. Rejected content can be edited and resubmitted.

Published Content

Navigate to Published (/admin/content) to see all live content. From here you can:

  • Search and filter published ceremonies and lineage records
  • Toggle visibility (hide content without deleting it) using the Unavailable status
  • Delete content permanently

Cinema Management

Navigate to Cinema (/admin/cinema) to manage the video streaming schedule.

ActionDescription
Create SessionAdd a new live or recorded cultural event: title, description, stream URL, scheduled date/time, and capacity.
Set as RecordedAfter a live session ends, update it with the VOD recording URL so it appears in the Recorded section for users.
Set UnavailableHide a recorded video from users without deleting it (useful for content under review).
Delete SessionPermanently remove a cinema entry and all associated bookings.

Tourism Management

Navigate to Tourism (/admin/tourism) to manage the sites shown on the public Tourism page. Unlike ceremonies and lineage records, tourist sites are admin-curated directly — there is no practitioner submission or review step.

1
Click "Add Site"

Fill in a name, description, category (Heritage Site, Lodge, Cultural Village, Nature Reserve, Restaurant, or Other), and an optional price range and photo.

2
Select related interests

Toggle the interest tags (Ceremonies, Royal Lineage, Music, Attire, Royal Culture, Spiritual) that best describe the site. These drive the "Recommended for you" matching on the public Tourism page.

3
Add a location

Type a place name to get suggestions and auto-fill coordinates. Sites without a location won't appear on the public map.

Analytics

Navigate to Analytics (/admin/analytics) to view platform usage trends: user registrations over time, ceremony views, most-engaged content, and booking volume. Charts are rendered using the platform's built-in visualisation library.

Ratings

Navigate to Ratings (/admin/ratings) to see all community ratings submitted across the platform. Features include:

  • Summary statistics: total ratings, average score, rating distribution
  • Filter by content type (Ceremonies / Lineage Records) or by specific content item
  • Paginated table of individual ratings with user, content name, star score, and review text

System Config

Navigate to Config (/admin/config) to manage global platform settings such as feature toggles, default language, and configurable UI text.

Audit Log

Navigate to Audit Log (/admin/audit) for a chronological record of all significant platform events: logins, content approvals, user role changes, deletions, and system configuration changes. Each entry includes the timestamp, acting user, and action performed.

ML Model

Navigate to ML Model (/admin/model) to view the current state of the recommendation model and initiate a retraining cycle when new content has been published. The model file is stored at server/ml/model.json.

🔴
Admin actions are permanent Deleting users, content, or cinema sessions cannot be undone. Always review the Audit Log before investigating suspected issues — it may show that a change was already made.

Language Support (EN / SiSwati)

The platform supports both English (EN) and SiSwati (SS) for the public-facing user interface. Language selection applies to navigation labels, buttons, headings, and placeholder text throughout the site.

Switching Language

The language toggle is a compact EN | SS pill selector visible in the top-right area of the navigation bar on desktop, and inside the mobile menu drawer. Click EN for English (blue highlight) or SS for SiSwati (red highlight). The change takes effect immediately without a page reload.

ℹ️
Content translations are separate from the UI language Switching UI language changes labels and navigation text. Ceremony and lineage record content is available in siSwati when the practitioner who submitted it filled in the siSwati translation fields. Both versions appear side-by-side on the detail pages when available.
